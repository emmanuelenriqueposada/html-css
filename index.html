Ingredents

  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup of milk
  • 1/4 cup of cheese
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Crack the eggs into a bowl.
  3. Add milk and whisk until combined.
  4. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Heat a non-stick skillet over medium heat.
  6. Pour the egg mixture into the skillet.
  7. Cook until the edges start to set, then sprinkle cheese on top.
  8. Fold the omelet in half and cook for another minute.
  9. Serve hot and enjoy!
